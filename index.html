About

An independent practice built on planning, not just filing.

I run an independent CPA practice in Goleta, serving clients across Santa Barbara and California's Central Coast. The focus is tax planning and preparation, entity strategy, and bookkeeping for individuals, trusts, and closely held businesses, with an emphasis on getting ahead of decisions rather than cleaning up after them.

My work spans S-corporation filings and reasonable-compensation strategy, startup and founder tax planning (Section 1202 QSBS, 83(b) elections, S-to-C conversions), HOA returns, household-employee payroll compliance, and QuickBooks cleanup. Before all of that, I served in the U.S. Navy, which is still how I approach a client's books: figure out what matters, do it right, and keep it simple.
